OLINK proteomics analysis

Workflow

Six stages, one traceable analysis

  1. 01

    Study design, manifest, and NPX file review

  2. 02

    Assay, sample, and limit-of-detection quality control

  3. 03

    Normalization assessment and batch-effect evaluation

  4. 04

    Differential protein abundance modeling with relevant covariates

  5. 05

    Pathway, network, biomarker, and multi-omics integration analyses

  6. 06

    Publication-ready figures, results tables, methods, and handoff

Built around the experiment—not a black box.

Tools and parameters are selected after reviewing study design, sample size, data quality, biological replicates, and the intended use of the results. Deliverables include methods documentation and analysis-ready outputs.
